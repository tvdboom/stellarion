# Asset credits

Stellarion uses the following NASA imagery in accordance with the
[NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
NASA does not endorse Stellarion.

## Asteroids

- `assets/images/asteroids/bennu.png` is adapted from the
  [Bennu Mosaic](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/bennu-mosaic/), credited to
  NASA/Goddard/University of Arizona.
- `assets/images/asteroids/eros.png` is adapted from the
  [NASA Scientific Visualization Studio true-color Eros model](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2061),
  credited to NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
- `assets/images/asteroids/gaspra.png` is adapted from
  [Ida and Gaspra](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/ida-and-gaspra/), credited to
  NASA/JPL/USGS.
- `assets/images/asteroids/mathilde.png` is adapted from
  [Two Views of Mathilde](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/two-views-of-mathilde/),
  credited to NASA/JPL/JHUAPL.

The game-ready files only remove the original black backgrounds, crop the objects,
and resize them onto transparent square canvases.
